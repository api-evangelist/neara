# Neara

> Neara builds a physics-enabled digital twin platform for electric utilities. It models every asset, clearance, and piece of surrounding vegetation in a single spatially precise, behaviourally accurate network model. Utilities use Neara to predict failures, mitigate wildfire and weather risk, accelerate network design, and optimise capital investment — at scale, in real time.

Neara is trusted by utilities across Australia, the UK, the US, and Europe. Customers include Essential Energy, Endeavour Energy, SA Power Networks, CenterPoint Energy, ESB Networks, and Scottish and Southern Electricity Networks (SSEN).
